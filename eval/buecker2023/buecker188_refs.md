## Verification method

1. **Included set** = the 188 `AuthorYYYY.pdf` filenames in Jansen's `LLM_coding_raw.xlsx` sheet `Buecker2023` (the PDFs actually fed to the LLMs).
2. **Ground truth** = the meta-analysis paper's own reference list; the paper states *“References marked with an asterisk indicate studies included in the meta-analysis.”* All 196 asterisked references were parsed (authors, year, title, inline DOI).
3. Each filename was matched to its asterisked reference (accent-folded surname + year, with author-token and fuzzy-surname fallbacks). **DOIs were taken from the paper**, then independently **resolved at Crossref** and the resolved title compared to the paper title (spot-check: first author matched the filename in 9/9 sampled DOIs).
4. Hard cases (parser block-merges, same-surname collisions, filename year-drift, spelling variants, wrapped-DOI mis-reads) were read line-by-line from the PDF and pinned in an audited `OVERRIDE_DOI` table in `build_refs.py`.
