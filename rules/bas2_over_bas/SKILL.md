---
name: bas2_over_bas
description: Prefer BAS-2 over BAS when both appear
activates_on: [text_pass, aggregator]
applies_to: [Assessment of BA]
---

When a paper mentions both the original Body Appreciation Scale (BAS) and the
revised BAS-2, and the primary analyses use BAS-2, record `BAS-2` as the
assessment of body appreciation. Only record `BAS` when BAS-2 is not used.
